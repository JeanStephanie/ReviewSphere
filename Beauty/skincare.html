Cleansers

Cleansers remove dirt, oil, and impurities, leaving the skin clean and refreshed without stripping its natural moisture.

Toners

Toners help remove residual impurities, balance skin's pH, and prepare it for better absorption of moisturizers or serums.

Exfoliators

Exfoliators remove dead skin cells, unclog pores, and promote smoother, brighter, and healthier-looking skin.

Serums

Serums are concentrated skincare treatments designed to target specific skin concerns like hydration, brightening, or anti-aging.

Moisturizers

Moisturizers hydrate and lock in moisture, keeping the skin soft, smooth, and protected from dryness.

Sunscreens

Sunscreens protect the skin from harmful UV rays, preventing sunburn, premature aging, and skin cancer.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How often should I wash my face?

Washing your face twice a day—morning and night—is ideal. Overwashing can strip natural oils, while under-washing can cause buildup and breakouts.

What is the best way to treat acne?

Use gentle cleansers, avoid harsh scrubbing, and incorporate products with salicylic acid or benzoyl peroxide. Staying hydrated and maintaining a healthy diet also helps.

How can I reduce dark circles under my eyes?

Apply a cold compress, use eye creams with vitamin C or retinol, and ensure you're getting enough sleep. Staying hydrated can also help reduce puffiness.

Should I apply sunscreen indoors?

Yes, UV rays can penetrate windows and cause skin damage. Wearing sunscreen daily, even indoors, helps protect your skin from premature aging.

Can I use face oils if I have oily skin?

Yes, lightweight, non-comedogenic oils like jojoba or rosehip can balance oil production and improve skin texture without clogging pores.
