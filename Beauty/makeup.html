Makeup Product Categories

Face Makeup

  • Foundation: Provides an even base and covers imperfections like blemishes and uneven skin tone. It comes in liquid, cream, powder, or stick form.
  • Concealer: Targets specific imperfections such as dark circles, pimples, or redness. Available in liquid, cream, or stick form.
  • Powder: Sets foundation and concealer in place, mattifies the face, and controls shine. It can be loose or pressed.
  • Blush: Adds color to the cheeks, giving the face a healthy flush. It comes in powder, cream, or liquid form.
  • Bronzer: Adds warmth to the face, creating a sun-kissed glow. It comes in powder or cream form and is used to contour the face.
  • Highlighter: Adds a radiant glow to the high points of the face like the cheekbones, nose bridge, and cupid's bow. Available in powder, cream, or liquid form.
  • Setting Spray/Powder: Locks makeup in place and controls oil, ensuring that makeup lasts longer. It can come in powder or mist form.

Eye Makeup

  • Eyeshadow: Adds color and depth to the eyelids. It comes in powder, cream, liquid, and pencil forms, with various finishes like matte, shimmer, and metallic.
  • Eyeliner: Defines the eyes by outlining the upper and lower lash lines. It can come in pencil, gel, liquid, or pen form.
  • Mascara: Darkens, lengthens, and volumizes the eyelashes. It can come in different formulas like lengthening, volumizing, and curling mascaras.
  • Eyebrow Products: Shapes and defines eyebrows. Common products include eyebrow pencils, powders, gels, and pomades.
  • False Lashes: Adds volume and length to natural eyelashes, creating a more dramatic look. They come in strip, individual, or flare forms.

Lip Makeup

  • Lipstick: Adds color to the lips in a variety of finishes such as matte, satin, glossy, or liquid. Available in bullet, liquid, or pencil forms.
  • Lip Gloss: Adds shine and a subtle tint of color to the lips. It’s often hydrating and can come in clear, tinted, or shimmery varieties.
  • Lip Liner: Defines the lips and prevents lipstick from feathering. It comes in pencil, gel, or liquid form and can help shape and contour the lips.
  • Lip Balm: Moisturizes and nourishes the lips. It can come in tinted or clear versions and provides protection from dryness.

Primers & Specialties

  • Face Primer: Prepares the skin for foundation, helping it to last longer and creating a smooth surface. It can be mattifying, hydrating, or pore-filling.
  • Eye Primer: Helps eyeshadow adhere better and prevents creasing. It can come in matte or shimmer formulas.
  • Contouring Kits: Used to sculpt and define facial features. Contour products come in cream, powder, or stick formulas.
  • Color Correctors: Neutralize specific skin issues like redness, dark circles, or dullness. Common colors include green, yellow, peach, and purple.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I choose the right foundation shade?

To find your perfect shade, test the foundation on your jawline in natural light. The right shade should seamlessly blend into your skin tone.

What is the best way to apply foundation?

Use a damp beauty sponge for a natural finish, a brush for full coverage, or your fingers for a lightweight look. Always blend well.

How can I make my lipstick last longer?

Exfoliate your lips, apply a lip primer or concealer, line your lips with a matching lip liner, and use a matte or long-wearing lipstick.

What’s the best way to remove makeup without damaging my skin?

Use a gentle makeup remover or micellar water, followed by a mild cleanser. Avoid scrubbing and always moisturize after cleansing.
