Types of Hair Maintenance Products

Shampoos

Shampoos cleanse the scalp and hair, removing dirt and product buildup. They hydrate and nourish the hair, improving texture and preventing dryness or breakage. Specialized shampoos address concerns like dandruff, hair loss, or color protection.

Hair Oils

Hair Serums

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I wash my hair?

It depends on your hair type. Oily hair may need washing every other day, while dry or curly hair can be washed 2-3 times a week to maintain moisture.

What is the best way to prevent hair damage?

Avoid excessive heat styling, use a heat protectant, trim split ends regularly, and opt for sulfate-free shampoos to maintain hair health.

How can I make my hair grow faster?

Maintain a balanced diet, massage your scalp to stimulate blood flow, use nourishing oils, and minimize heat and chemical treatments.

Should I use conditioner every time I wash my hair?

Yes, conditioner helps keep your hair hydrated and smooth. If you have fine hair, use a lightweight conditioner to avoid buildup.

Can I use oil on my hair every day?

Using oil daily can weigh down your hair, especially if it's fine or oily. Instead, apply oil 1-2 times a week before shampooing for nourishment.
