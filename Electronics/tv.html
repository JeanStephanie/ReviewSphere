CATEGORIES OF TV

Display Technology

  • LED (Light Emitting Diode): Provides bright and energy-efficient displays.
  • OLED (Organic Light Emitting Diode): Offers high contrast ratios and deep blacks.
  • QLED (Quantum Dot LED): Uses quantum dots for enhanced color and brightness.
  • LCD (Liquid Crystal Display): Common and cost-effective technology.
  • Plasma: Older technology, less common now but known for good color accuracy.

Screen Size

  • Small (Under 40 inches): Ideal for bedrooms or small spaces.
  • Medium (40-55 inches): Suitable for most living rooms.
  • Large (Above 55 inches): Best for home theaters and large rooms.

Resolution

  • HD (720p): Basic high-definition resolution, suitable for small screens.
  • Full HD (1080p): Standard resolution for most modern TVs.
  • 4K (Ultra HD): Offers four times the resolution of Full HD, providing sharper images.
  • 8K (Ultra High Definition): Provides the highest resolution available, with incredible detail.

Smart TVs

  • Smart TVs are televisions that come with built-in internet connectivity, allowing users to access streaming services like Netflix, YouTube, Hulu, and more, directly from the TV.
  • They often feature apps,voice control, and support for casting from mobile devices.
  • hey also offer features like browsing the web, gaming, and even controlling smart home devices.
  • Equipped with internet connectivity and streaming apps for a versatile viewing experience.

    • Refresh Rate

    • 60Hz: Standard refresh rate for most TVs, suitable for regular viewing.
    • 120Hz: Provides smoother motion for sports and action scenes.
    • 240Hz: Offers the smoothest motion, ideal for gaming and fast-paced content.

    Sound Quality

    • Built-in soundbars: Provides enhanced audio quality without needing external speakers.
    • Dolby Atmos support: Delivers immersive 3D audio for a theater-like experience.
    • Advanced audio technologies: Includes various enhancements for superior sound quality.

    FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

    Why is my TV screen blurry?

    Blurriness can be caused by a poor cable connection, a low-quality signal, or incorrect settings. Check the cable connections and make sure the resolution is set to match your TV's capabilities.

    How can I improve the sound quality of my TV?

    You can improve sound quality by adjusting the TV’s audio settings, such as treble, bass, and balance. Additionally, connecting external speakers or a soundbar can enhance your audio experience.

    Why is my TV not turning on?

    Check if the TV is plugged in and if the power outlet is working. Try a different remote or press the power button on the TV itself. If the issue persists, there may be a problem with the power supply.

    What is HDMI ARC, and why should I use it?

    HDMI ARC (Audio Return Channel) allows you to send audio from the TV back to a soundbar or home theater system using a single HDMI cable. It simplifies cable management and improves audio quality.

    How do I connect my TV to Wi-Fi?

    Navigate to the network settings in your TV's menu and select your Wi-Fi network. Enter the password to establish the connection. If your TV doesn’t detect Wi-Fi, ensure your router is functioning properly.

    What is the difference between LED, OLED, and QLED TVs?

    LED TVs use LED backlighting to enhance brightness, while OLED TVs offer better contrast and deeper blacks thanks to individual pixel lighting. QLED TVs, a type of LED, use Quantum Dot technology for more vivid colors and brightness.
