Types of Vacuum Cleaners

Upright Vacuum

Upright vacuums are designed for efficient, deep cleaning, offering powerful suction and easy maneuverability across carpets, rugs, and hard floors. With a large cleaning path and upright design, they allow for quick and thorough coverage of larger areas.

Canister Vacuum

Canister vacuums are versatile and lightweight, offering powerful suction for deep cleaning on both carpets and hard floors. With a separate canister and hose, they provide greater maneuverability and are ideal for reaching tight spaces, stairs, and upholstery, while advanced filtration ensures cleaner air..

Robot Vacuum

Robot vacuums offer automated cleaning with minimal effort, navigating your home to remove dirt, dust, and debris on carpets and hard floors. Equipped with smart sensors, they efficiently clean around furniture and obstacles, and many models can be controlled remotely via apps.

Handheld Vacuum

Handheld vacuums are compact, lightweight, and perfect for quick clean-ups of small messes on surfaces like upholstery, stairs, and car interiors. With powerful suction in a portable design, they efficiently capture dust, dirt, and debris, making them ideal for tight spaces and hard-to-reach areas.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I clean the filter of my vacuum cleaner?

Remove the filter from the vacuum, tap off loose dirt, and rinse it under running water. Let it dry completely before reinstalling.

Why is my vacuum cleaner losing suction power?

This could be due to a clogged filter, a full dustbin, or a blockage in the hose. Check these areas and clean or empty them as needed.

What should I do if my vacuum cleaner overheats?

Turn off the vacuum and unplug it. Check for clogs in the hose or brush roll and ensure the filters are clean. Allow the motor to cool before using it again.

How often should I replace the vacuum cleaner bag?

Replace the vacuum cleaner bag when it is about two-thirds full or if you notice a drop in suction power. Using the bag beyond this point can reduce performance.

Can I vacuum wet surfaces with my vacuum cleaner?

Most standard vacuum cleaners are not designed for wet surfaces. Use a wet-dry vacuum cleaner for such tasks to avoid damaging the unit.
