Buying Guide for Scooter

When choosing between an electric and a non-electric scooter, there are several factors to consider based on your preferences, needs, and intended use. Below is a comprehensive buying guide for both types:


Electric Scooters:


  • Battery Life and Range: Battery life varies by model, typically ranging from 250Wh to 1000Wh. Higher Wh means longer ranges (e.g., 30-50 miles). Charging time can range from 4 to 12 hours.

  • Motor Power: Motor power is measured in watts. A 250W motor is suitable for flat terrains, while 500W+ motors are better for hilly areas.

  • Speed:Look for a scooter with speeds that fit your needs (e.g., 15 mph for casual use, 20-25 mph for longer commutes).

  • Weight: Electric scooters are heavier (typically 25-40 lbs) due to the battery and motor. If portability is important, choose a lightweight model.

  • Braking System: Disc brakes and drum brakes are common, but electric scooters also have regenerative braking, which recharges the battery during braking.

  • Wheels and Suspension: Larger wheels (8 inches or more) are ideal for smoother rides, and suspension is important for comfort, especially if you plan to ride on rough terrain.

  • Build Quality and Materials: Opt for a durable frame made of steel or aluminum. Look for weather-resistant features if you plan to use it outdoors in various conditions.

    • Non Electric Scooters:


  • Deck Size and Material: A wider deck offers better comfort and stability. Materials like aluminum or steel are durable.

  • Wheels: Larger wheels (100mm or more) provide a smoother ride over uneven surfaces.

  • Handlebars: Adjustable handlebars are great for users of different heights. Ensure they are ergonomic and offer a good grip.

  • Weight:Non-electric scooters are generally lighter, usually between 7 to 15 lbs. Consider the portability if you need to carry or store it.

  • Brake System:Common types are foot brakes or hand-operated brakes. Make sure they offer good responsiveness and safety.

  • Suspension:Some higher-end models offer front or rear suspension to help absorb bumps and provide a smoother ride.

  • Style and Design:There are various designs, including kick scooters for kids, commuter scooters for adults, and stunt scooters for trick riding. Choose based on your preference.

By evaluating these factors carefully,this guide should help you make an informed decision and find the perfect scooter for your needs.
